Yepper local video helper
=========================

This helper processes the video on your computer. The video itself is never
uploaded to Yepper or another processing service. Only the public ad image
URLs are fetched from Cloudinary.

Requirements
------------
- Node.js 20 or newer
- FFmpeg and ffprobe installed and available on PATH
- Internet access to download the selected public Cloudinary badge image(s)

Run
---
1. Download local-video-helper.mjs and save it in a folder.
2. Open a terminal in that folder.
3. Use your input video path, output path, selected percentage slots, and each
   selected slot's Cloudinary image URL:

   node local-video-helper.mjs --input "my-video.mp4" --output "my-video-with-ads.mkv" --slots 8pct,25pct --badge "8pct=https://res.cloudinary.com/ACCOUNT/image/upload/BADGE1.png" --badge "25pct=https://res.cloudinary.com/ACCOUNT/image/upload/BADGE2.png"

   Or download each badge image first and pass its local path:

   node local-video-helper.mjs --input "my-video.mp4" --output "my-video-with-ads.mkv" --slots 8pct,25pct --badge "8pct=badge-8pct.png" --badge "25pct=badge-25pct.png"

The slot timestamps are calculated from the source duration. Each badge is
shown for 15 seconds at bottom-right. For every selected slot, the helper copies
the untouched sections, re-encodes only the badge section with the source video
codec/profile/pixel format/frame rate/bitrate when available, and joins the
sections with FFmpeg's concat demuxer. Audio is stream-copied.

The helper writes a Matroska (.mkv) output so common source codecs can remain
unchanged. Check that the selected codec can be encoded by your local FFmpeg.
The helper may fail for unsupported or unusual codecs, encrypted files,
overlapping badge windows, or media with incompatible stream parameters. Keep
the original video; processing never overwrites it.

Limitations
-----------
- Cuts made with stream copy are keyframe-aligned. For source videos whose
  keyframes do not line up with a requested percentage timestamp, a cut or
  badge can begin slightly before or after the exact timestamp.
- The final file keeps the source video codec and copies audio, but this
  segment-based method cannot guarantee bit-for-bit continuity at every join.
- The browser route has a conservative 256 MiB input cap. Actual capacity is
  lower or higher depending on browser, available memory, codec, and output
  size. Use this local helper for larger videos.
